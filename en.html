                    Closed beta application

                    First 20 households. You supply a synthetic or non-sensitive profile; no account numbers, no custodian access, no real client funds.
                    1. Your country and reporting currency.
                    2. The decision you are actually stuck on, in your own words.
                    3. The closest profile: dual-income with a mortgage / single earner with dependents / pre-retirement couple / business owner with illiquid wealth / irregular income / multi-currency household.
                    This static preview does not submit anything. The interactive console records applications locally through POST /api/v0/feedback.
                    What the beta measures
                    Whether the family recognises the pain in the verdict, whether the first verdict is trusted, whether ABSTAIN is accepted as honest rather than broken, and whether a paid household returns in month two.
                    What is never collected
                    Bank or broker credentials, account numbers, custody access, real holdings feeds, or identity documents. Only a synthetic or non-sensitive profile.
